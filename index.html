How FoodShare Works

A rapid four-step pipeline ensuring surplus cooked meals and fresh food reach vulnerable beneficiaries before expiry.

01

1. Donors List Surplus

Restaurants, banquets, and bakeries list surplus food with quantity, expiry time limit, and pickup address.

02

2. NGOs Claim Food

Verified relief NGOs browse available donations by category and location, reserving suitable provisions for distribution.

03

3. Volunteers Transport

Community volunteers accept delivery assignments, pick up food from donors, and transport it to the designated NGO shelter.

04

4. Meals Distributed

Food is served to families in need before expiry, preventing waste and ensuring community nutrition.

Role-Based Ecosystem

Designed for Every Participant

Tailored tools for donors, NGOs, and volunteers to collaborate seamlessly on hunger relief.

Food Donors

Restaurants, Caterers & Hostels

Turn edible end-of-day surplus into community goodwill rather than landfill emissions.

  • Instant donation form with automatic validation
  • Track donation lifecycle (Available → Claimed → Delivered)
  • Specify storage guidelines, dietary badges & expiry

Relief NGOs

Shelters, Soup Kitchens & Trusts

Source nutritious, free food supplies for orphanages, shelters, and slum community kitchens.

  • Filter by food category, urgency, and pickup location
  • One-click claiming with beneficiary allocation
  • "Expiring Soon" priority alerts (< 24 hrs)

Volunteers

Local Couriers & Helpers

Connect donor kitchens with NGO dining halls by providing local pickup and delivery.

  • View claimed delivery assignments in real-time
  • Update transit status: Picked Up → Delivered
  • Track personal delivery history and community impact
