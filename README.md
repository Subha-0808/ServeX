
# FoodShare – Surplus Food Donation and Distribution Platform

## Problem Statement

Large amounts of edible surplus food from restaurants, bakeries, hostels, supermarkets, and events are wasted every day due to the lack of an efficient system to connect food donors with NGOs and volunteers. At the same time, many people face food insecurity and do not have access to timely food distribution.

## Proposed Solution

FoodShare is a web-based platform that connects food donors, NGOs, and volunteers to facilitate the collection and distribution of surplus food to people in need.

The platform allows donors to list surplus food, NGOs to browse and claim available donations, and volunteers to coordinate pickups and deliveries. It aims to reduce food wastage and make surplus food more accessible to communities in need.

## Objectives

- Reduce food wastage by redistributing surplus food.
- Connect food donors with NGOs and volunteers.
- Facilitate the timely collection and delivery of food.
- Improve coordination between donors, NGOs, and volunteers.
- Encourage community participation in food donation activities.

## Key Features

- **Role-Based Login:** Separate access for Donors, NGOs, Volunteers, and Admins.
- **Food Donation Listing:** Donors can upload food details, quantity, expiry time, and pickup location.
- **Donation Claiming:** NGOs can browse available donations and claim suitable food listings.
- **Volunteer Coordination:** Volunteers can manage assigned pickups and deliveries.
- **Status Updates:** Track donations through different stages of collection and delivery.
- **Expiry Monitoring:** Identify donations that are approaching their expiry time.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Manage donation listings and monitor platform activity.
- **Impact Statistics:** View donation history, meals saved, and food waste reduced.
- **Ratings and Feedback:** Allow donors, NGOs, and volunteers to provide feedback.

## User Roles

### Donor
- Add surplus food donation details.
- Specify food quantity, expiry time, and pickup location.
- View the status of donations.

### NGO
- Browse available food donations.
- View food details and expiry information.
- Claim suitable donations.
- Track claimed donations.

### Volunteer
- View assigned pickups and deliveries.
- Coordinate food collection and distribution.
- Update delivery status.

### Admin
- Manage donation listings and user activities.
- Monitor donations and platform usage.
- View donation statistics and impact reports.
- Manage platform records.

## Application Workflow

1. Donors list surplus food along with its quantity, expiry time, and pickup location.
2. NGOs browse the available donations and claim suitable listings.
3. Volunteers coordinate the pickup and delivery of the claimed food.
4. Donation statuses are updated as the food moves through the distribution process.
5. Admins monitor the platform and view donation statistics.

## Current Implementation

FoodShare is being developed as a web-based prototype using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The prototype uses browser LocalStorage to store donation details and status updates.

The initial implementation focuses on food donation listings, NGO claiming, volunteer delivery tracking, and basic administrative features. Advanced functionality such as secure authentication, centralized data storage, and real-time notifications can be added in future versions.

## Future Enhancements

- Secure login and role-based authorization.
- Backend and database integration.
- Real-time notifications for donation claims and delivery updates.
- Google Maps integration for location-based food listings.
- NGO verification and admin approval.
- Ratings and feedback system.
- Detailed reports on meals saved and food waste reduced.

## Expected Outcome

FoodShare aims to provide a common platform for food donors, NGOs, and volunteers to coordinate surplus food collection and distribution. By improving communication and coordination, the platform seeks to reduce edible food wastage and make food distribution more efficient.
